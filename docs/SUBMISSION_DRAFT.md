## Title
**THE FINAL REHEARSAL**

## Tagline
*Your past is your greatest weapon.*

## Short description (for the jam card)
An ancient being has imprisoned time itself. Every spell you cast leaves an echo of your past that keeps casting it. Build formations of your former selves, turn stolen moments against their master, and synchronize your magic to break the cycle. One chamber. One boss. Four echoes. Five minutes.

## Page text

**Time has rehearsed this moment before. This time, change the ending.**

You stand in a forbidden astronomical chamber before **The Watcher**: an ivory-masked being who collects moments of time and has watched this fight happen countless times.

You have learned his own forbidden magic. **Every spell you cast leaves an echo of you behind**, and that echo keeps repeating your spell every four seconds, from exactly where you stood.

- **Imprint** (left click): cast a spell. It leaves an echo.
- **Convergence** (E): command your echoes to strike as one. Two, three, or four past versions of you, from as many directions as you dare.

The Watcher fights back with your own tools. He repeats his attacks through a crimson afterimage of himself. He steals your echoes and turns them against you, unless you turn them back on him. He replays your own footsteps. And when you think you've won, he asks whether you really believed this was the first time.

Three phases. A boss who learns how you play. A finale built from the formation *you* made.

### Controls
| | |
|---|---|
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Aim (click once to capture the mouse) |
| Left click | Imprint: cast, and leave an echo |
| E | Convergence: your echoes strike together |
| Space | Jump |
| Esc | Free the cursor (for the sound panel and retry button) |

### How to play
1. Open the World: **[rgl10.dcl.eth link after deploy]**, in the Decentraland desktop app (recommended) or browser.
2. Click to aim, and cast at the Watcher.
3. Move, and cast again. Watch what you left behind.
4. When your echoes' runes glow gold, press **E**.

Everything else you'll discover in the fight.

### Features
- An original core mechanic: your past spells become a battlefield formation you build and synchronize.
- A boss with personality: voiced, adaptive, with distinct wind-ups for every attack.
- Corruption reversal: a stolen echo can become the weapon that hurts him most.
- A three-act structure: a false victory, three seals and a finale fired from your own formation.
- Fair by design: every attack is telegraphed and every attack has a counter. Guided mode is available after a defeat.
- Ranks from Apprentice to **The Unforeseen**, six achievements, and a personal formation map at the end.
- An original adaptive orchestral score and sound design.
